# Trench Run Blitz

I want to mock up a video game. It's called Trench Warfare. It's going to have three phases. The first phase, you use your Imperial credits to buy your units. The second phase is a battle in space between the units. The third phase is an attack on the Death Star. The two teams are the Resistance and the Empire. In the first phase, the Resistance can either buy X-wings or Y-wings. They have 20 Imperial credits. The X-wings cost two credits, the Y-wings cost three credits. The Empire can either buy TIE fighters, super TIE fighters, or cannons. The TIE fighters cost two, the super TIE fighters cost five, and the cannons cost one. They can spend no more than the 20 Imperial credits. In the second phase, it's going to be the X-wings and Y-wings versus the TIE fighters and super TIE fighters, and they're going to go in rounds. The X-wings are going to attack on a four, five, or six roll of the dice. The Y-wings are going to attack and hit if it's a six roll of the dice. The TIE fighters are going to attack on a four, five, or six. The super TIE fighters are going to attack on a three, four, five, and six. So the X-wings and Y-wings roll first, and then the TIE fighters and super TIE fighters roll back. The units are taken off the board, and they keep going until there are no more TIE fighters or super TIE fighters remaining. If all the X-wings and Y-wings get destroyed, the Empire wins. If any X-wings and Y-wings continue, they go into the third phase of the game, the trench run. The cannons of the Empire attack at only a six. So if it's a roll of a six of the dice, they get to fire in two rounds. If they get any sixes in the first round, that number of X-wing and/or Y-wings are destroyed. Same thing for the second round. And then any remaining X-wing and Y-wing get to attack the Death Star. The X-wings will bomb at a roll of a six. The Y-wings bomb at a three, four, five, or six. Any bomb that hits destroys the Death Star, and the Resistance wins. Every time a player's units are destroyed, they get to choose which unit. So for example, if the Empire gets two hits, then the Resistance gets to choose how many X-wings or Y-wings go off. Ask if you have any questions.
